Optimizer Experiments: 4-bit Symmetric Quantization

Setup

Matrix: 2048 by 2048, standard normal times 0.02.

Quantization: 4-bit symmetric per tensor.

Batch size: 10. Train inputs: 100.

Objective: minimize MSE of quantized output versus original output.

Hyperparameter explanations

Adam lr=1e-4: Learning rate 0.0001. Betas 0.9 and 0.95 control momentum. 100 steps. Baseline Adam. Gives 4.36x improvement.

Adam lr=1e-3 clip=1.0: Learning rate 0.001, ten times higher, with gradient clipping at 1.0. The higher rate causes overshooting. Gradient clipping does not help. Result is worse than baseline.

Adam lr=1e-4 cosine: Same as baseline but learning rate decays from 0.0001 to near zero following a cosine curve over 100 steps. This is the best. The decaying rate lets the optimizer settle into a better minimum.

SGD momentum 0.9: Learning rate 0.01 with momentum 0.9. Momentum helps but the fixed rate is too coarse. Only 1.57x improvement.

Adam lr=1e-4 300 steps: Same as baseline but 300 steps instead of 100. More steps do not help. The optimizer plateaus after 40 steps and then slightly worsens. Early stopping at 40 steps would be better.

RMSprop lr=1e-4: Learning rate 0.0001. Uses running average of squared gradients. Decent at 3.12x but not as good as Adam.

Key lessons

  • The learning rate schedule matters more than the optimizer. Cosine annealing gave 15.83x, plain Adam gave 4.36x.
  • More steps do not help. The optimizer converges in about 40 steps.
  • Gradient clipping with a high learning rate makes things worse.
  • SGD is not effective for this task.
  • RMSprop is acceptable but Adam is better.
